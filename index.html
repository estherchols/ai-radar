About

Before Stanford I was the first corporate venture manager at Thai Union, worked on more than 30 cross-border deals at The Quant Group, and started out as a management trainee at L'Oréal. I graduated as valedictorian from Thammasat University on the King Scholarship.

Outside class I lead professional development for Women in Management at the GSB, and I founded a support group for women in business back in Bangkok. I want women to stand on their own in whatever work looks like after AI, and I think that starts with access to opportunity and capital.
